Citation discipline

Every citation in this book is a pointer, something like "see Feathers for the full dependency-breaking technique," not a displacing summary. The books exist; if this book replaced reading them, it would do the authors and the reader a disservice. Only actual books appear here; the Python documentation, PEPs, and web articles a chapter cites stay in that chapter.
